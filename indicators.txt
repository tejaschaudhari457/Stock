#What is a Technical Indicator? (Technical View)

#A technical indicator is a mathematical calculation based on:
Price
Volume
Time
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
#It is plotted on price charts or below charts to help

Identify trend
Find entry & exit
Measure momentum
Judge strength or weakness
Reduce emotional trading
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Type                  | Purpose                        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Trend Indicators      | Direction of market            |
| Momentum Indicators   | Speed of price movement        |
| Volatility Indicators | Market expansion / contraction |
| Volume Indicators     | Strength behind price          |
| Support & Resistance  | Price reaction zones           |
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
